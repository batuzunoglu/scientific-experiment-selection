# Research Charter (draft)

**Working title:** Learning Reliable Experiment-Selection Policies for Scientific Model Adaptation.

**Goal:** Determine whether cost-aware, sequential choices of physical laboratory experiments can efficiently restore or verify scientific model reliability under experimental distribution shift.

**Initial research testbed:** Ce–ARS/PFAS spectra from multiple experimental batches, subject to source file and metadata audit. No datasets are included here.

**Candidate baselines:** random selection, standard design of experiments, uncertainty/diversity selection, appropriate Bayesian design/acquisition.

**Research directions:** structured non-language RL policy; tool-using small language agent; SFT/RL post-training only when realistic, independently testable training environments exist.

**Deliverables:** reproducible benchmark/code, laboratory prospective validation, figures linked to verified runs, PhD proposal, manuscript, documented internship-ready portfolio.

**Open decisions:** label/prediction target, action space, sample/batch splits, adaptation algorithm, QC as an action, experiment costs, dataset permissions, prospective lab protocol.

**Not in scope:** general autonomous robot laboratory, universal chemistry foundation-model training, claims of a new method before baselines establish the gap.
